Step 1 · Complete Your TQS Self-Reflection

Rate how consistently each practice shows up in your teaching right now, and add notes where useful. Your honest ratings — especially the lower ones — become the foundation of your growth goals. Nothing here is an evaluation.

Format: 2025-26, 2026-27, … — sets all the dates in your plan (September start → June end).

Step 4 · Generate Your Draft TPGP

Choose your options and generate. Your plan will follow the Third Schools template and Alberta's TPGP expectations — SMART goals tied to the six TQS competencies, with objectives, strategies, measures, and resources.

Your key is never stored. It is used only for this generation and sent directly to Anthropic. If you set the ANTHROPIC_API_KEY environment variable before launching, leave this blank.
