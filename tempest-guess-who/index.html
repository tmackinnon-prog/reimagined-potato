How to Play

  • Each round you get a mystery card: a quote from the play or a description of how a character behaves.
  • Click the matching character on the board. The clock is running.
  • A wrong guess knocks that card down and adds +3 seconds.
  • Stuck? Ask a yes/no question, like in Guess Who. Every card that doesn't fit falls down. Each question costs +2 seconds.
  • The faces and the suspects on the board change every round, so you have to read the names.
  • The lowest total time wins. Your score goes on this computer's leaderboard.
