⚙️Setup & Installation

Quick Start (30 seconds)

Navigate to the folder: Desktop → Claude Projects → Teacher Tools → Advanced Syllabus Generator
Find the file: Look for index.html
Double-click: Opens in your default browser immediately
Start creating: Begin filling in Phase 1
✅ That's it! No installation, no setup, no accounts needed. The application is ready to use immediately.

System Requirements

Operating System Windows, Mac, Linux Any OS with a web browser
Browser Chrome, Edge, Firefox Safari works but with limitations

  • Best: Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge (full functionality, file uploads work smoothly)
  • Good: Firefox (fully supported, identical functionality)
  • Limited: Safari on Mac (may have issues with file downloads)

First Time Opening

When you open the application for the first time:

  1. Page loads: You see a blue header with "Advanced Syllabus Generator"
  2. Progress bar appears: Shows 6 numbered circles at the top (1-6)
  3. Phase 1 displays: "👤 Instructor & Course Information" form appears
  4. You're ready: Start filling in the required fields (marked with *)
💡 Tip: Your data is saved automatically to your browser's local storage. You don't need to click "Save" — the application remembers everything as you type.

📖Complete Usage Guide

Understanding the Workflow

The application is divided into 5 phases, each building on the previous one. There is no calendar or class-schedule step — this generator is built for asynchronous / self-paced courses that don't meet at fixed times.

Phase 1 → Phase 2 → Phase 3 → Phase 4 → Phase 5
You progress forward with the Next → button, and can go back with ← Back to edit previous sections.

Phase 1: Instructor & Course Information

This phase collects the basic information about you and your course that appears on the syllabus cover.

Required Fields (marked with *)

FieldExampleNotes
Instructor Title Mr., Mrs., Ms., Dr., Prof. Select from dropdown. Displayed as "Mr. Smith" on syllabus
Instructor Name Smith Last name or full name. Appears in contact section
Course Title Biology 30, Chemistry 20, English 11 Official course name. Required to proceed
School Year 2025–2026 Use en-dash (–) or hyphen (-). Auto-filled with current year
Course Start Date September 1, 2025 Click calendar icon to select. Must be before end date
Course End Date June 15, 2026 Click calendar icon to select. Used for pacing calculations

Optional Fields (but recommended)

Course Code: BIO 30, CHEM 20 (helps with course identification)
Course Emoji: 🧬, ⚗️, 📐 (adds visual appeal, single emoji only)

Course Overview: 1-2 sentence description of what the course covers
Contact Info: Email and/or phone number
Office Hours: When students can reach you (e.g., "Monday & Wednesday 3-4 PM")
⚠️ Validation Rule: All fields marked with * must be filled before you can advance to Phase 2. If you try to click "Next →" with missing fields, you'll see an error message telling you what's required.

Uploading your Program of Studies saves time by automatically extracting key information:

  • Program of Studies: Extracts units, learning outcomes, weight percentages

Locate your document (PDF, Word, Excel, or CSV file)
Click the upload area (it says "Drag & drop your Program of Studies")
Select your file from the file browser that opens

Phase 3: Curriculum & Grading

Status: Required validation — Units and grade categories must total 100%

Part A: Units of Study

This section defines the major topics/units students will study, their importance, and estimated time.

Click "+ Add Unit" button
A new row appears with three fields:
  • Unit Name: "Cell Biology", "Genetics", "Evolution"
  • % of Grade: 25 (unit weight in course)
  • Weeks (Est.): 3 (approximate duration)
Fill in all units you'll teach
IMPORTANT: All percentages must total exactly 100%

⚠️ Validation: If percentages don't equal 100%, you'll see an error when trying to advance. Adjust values until they total exactly 100%.

Part B: Grade Categories

Define how students' grades are calculated. Example:

Category% of Final Grade

Unit Tests25%

Click "+ Add Category"
Enter category name (e.g., "Participation")
Enter percentage of final grade (e.g., 10)
Add all categories until they total 100%
⚠️ Validation: Grade categories must also total 100%. The app will not let you proceed until this is correct.

Set the percentage thresholds for each letter grade. Default values are provided:

Part D: Course Policies

Add detailed policies that students need to know. For each field, write a clear paragraph (2-3 sentences):

  • Late Work Policy: When work is considered late, what penalties apply, if any
  • Attendance & Absences: Expectations for attendance, how absences affect grades
  • Academic Integrity & Cheating: What constitutes cheating, consequences
  • Classroom Conduct: Rules, expectations, consequences for disruption
  • AI Use Policy: Your stance on generative AI (ChatGPT, etc.) in your course
💡 Tip: Be specific. Instead of "No cheating," write: "Cheating on quizzes or exams will result in a zero on that assessment and a meeting with the principal. Repeated violations may result in suspension."

Phase 4: Review & Customize

Status: No validation gates. Fully optional.

What Happens in This Phase

A live preview of your syllabus is automatically generated showing all the information you've entered so far. You can:

  • Review everything at a glance
  • Make sure all information is correct
  • Add optional sections to enhance your syllabus

Optional Sections You Can Add

SectionPurposeExample
Learning Outcomes What students will be able to do by end of course "Students will understand cell structure, function, and division"
Expectations for Success What students should do to succeed "Come to class prepared, complete assignments on time, ask questions"
Big Idea / Essential Question Overarching theme of the course "How do living things adapt to their environments?"
Required Technology Software, devices, platforms needed "Google Classroom, Desmos, Zoom"
Accessibility & Accommodations How you support diverse learners "I work with Special Education to provide accommodations"
✅ No pressure: All of these sections are optional. Your syllabus is complete without them. Add them only if they add value to your course.

Phase 5: Export Your Syllabus

Status: Final phase. Choose and download your preferred format(s).

Export Options

🌐 HTML (Canvas-Ready)

Best for: Pasting directly into Canvas
What you get: Professional HTML file with inline styling
How to use:
  1. Download the HTML file to your computer
  2. Open the file in any browser to preview
  3. Copy all the content (Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C)
  4. In Canvas: Course → Pages → Create New Page
  5. Click HTML Editor button
  6. Paste the content (Ctrl+V)
  7. Click Save

📄 DOCX (Word Document)

Best for: Professional editing and printing
What you get: Professionally formatted Word document with color-coded sections
How to use:
  1. Download the DOCX file
  2. Open in Microsoft Word, Google Docs, or LibreOffice
  3. Edit as needed (add images, change fonts, customize)
  4. Print or share with colleagues/students

📊 CSV (Spreadsheet)

Best for: Pacing guide and lesson planning
What you get: Spreadsheet with unit names, percentages, estimated weeks, start/end weeks
How to use:
  1. Download the CSV file
  2. Open in Excel or Google Sheets
  3. Use as a planning document
  4. Share with colleagues for curriculum coordination

📦 ZIP (All Formats)

Best for: Complete backup and flexible use
What you get: Single ZIP file containing HTML, DOCX, and CSV
How to use:
  1. Download the ZIP file
  2. Right-click → Extract All
  3. All three formats appear in a folder
  4. Use whichever format you need

What's Included in Every Export?

  • ✅ Complete syllabus with all course information
  • ✅ Instructor details and contact information
  • ✅ Grade categories and letter grade scale
  • ✅ Units of study and course pacing
  • ✅ All course policies
  • ✅ Learning outcomes and expectations (if you added them)
  • ✅ Course overview and required materials

Can I Export Multiple Times?

Yes! You can:

  • Export one format, then come back and export another
  • Make changes in Phase 4 and re-export
  • Export as many times as you want
  • Keep different versions for different purposes
✅ You're done! Click "Start Over" to create another syllabus, or close your browser. Your data is saved in your browser, so you can come back and make changes anytime.

❓Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data saved? ▼
Your data is saved in your browser's local storage. This means:
  • You don't need to click "Save" — it saves automatically as you type
  • Your data stays on your computer — never sent to any server
  • If you close your browser — your data is still there when you reopen the page
  • If you clear browser cache — your data will be deleted (so export before clearing cache)
Can I use this on multiple computers? ▼
Yes, but with a caveat: The app works on any computer, but your data is local to each browser. So:
  • If you use it on Computer A, your data is stored on Computer A only
  • On Computer B, you'd start fresh (or import the exported file)
  • Solution: Export your syllabus (ZIP file) and keep it backed up. You can import it later if needed
What happens if my browser crashes? ▼
Don't panic! Your data is saved in browser storage automatically. When you reopen the app:
  • All your data will still be there (assuming you didn't clear the cache)
  • You can continue from where you left off
  • Best practice: Export your syllabus every few phases as a backup
Can I edit the exported syllabus? ▼
Yes, absolutely!
  • DOCX: Open in Word/Docs and edit freely
  • HTML: Open in Word and edit, or paste into Canvas and edit there
  • CSV: Open in Excel and modify as needed

The exports are just starting points. Feel free to customize them after download.

Do I have to use all 5 phases? ▼
Mostly yes, but with flexibility:
  • Phase 1 (Setup): Required — forms the foundation
  • Phase 2 (Documents): Optional — skip if you prefer manual entry
  • Phase 3 (Curriculum): Required — defines your course structure
  • Phase 4 (Review): Optional — but useful to review before export
  • Phase 5 (Export): Required — how you get your syllabus
Can I go back and edit earlier phases? ▼
Yes! Use the "← Back" button to return to any previous phase. You can:
  • Edit any information you've entered
  • Add or remove units and grade categories
  • Change dates, policies, etc.
  • Then click "Next →" to proceed forward again

Your changes are saved automatically.

How do I share my syllabus with students? ▼
Multiple options:
  • Canvas: Upload HTML version to a Canvas page
  • Email: Send DOCX file via email
  • PDF: Print DOCX to PDF and email/share
  • Google Classroom: Upload file to assignment
  • School website: Post HTML version on your teacher page
Is my data private? ▼
Yes, completely.
  • No servers: Your data never leaves your computer
  • No accounts: You don't create an account or log in
  • No tracking: No analytics, no cookies, no data collection
  • No internet required: Works 100% offline after initial load
  • Local only: Data stored in your browser's local storage
How do I start over with a new syllabus? ▼
Two options:
  • Option 1: At the end of Phase 5, click "Start Over" button
  • Option 2: Close the browser tab and reopen the application (creates new session)

Warning: Starting over will erase your current work unless you export first!

🔧Troubleshooting Guide

❌ Application won't open / blank page

Problem: Click index.html but page doesn't load, or shows blank white screen
Solutions:
  1. Wait 3-5 seconds — sometimes it takes a moment to load
  2. Refresh the page (F5 or Ctrl+R)
  3. Try a different browser (Chrome or Edge recommended)
  4. Clear browser cache and reload
  5. Disable browser extensions that might block JavaScript
  6. Try opening in incognito/private window
If still not working: The file may be corrupted. Re-download the application.

❌ File upload not working

Problem: Can't upload PDF, Excel, or CSV files in Phase 2
Solutions:
  1. Use a local server: Direct file access (file://) has security restrictions 
    python -m http.server 8080
# Then open http://localhost:8080
  2. Try dragging the file instead of clicking "Select File"
  3. Check file format — only PDF, DOCX, CSV, XLSX are supported
  4. Try a different browser (Chrome works best)
  5. Check file size — very large files may fail (stay under 10 MB)
  6. If a PDF, make sure it's not password-protected
If uploads aren't critical: Just skip Phase 2 and enter data manually in Phase 3

❌ Can't advance to next phase

Problem: "Next →" button is disabled or shows error message
Solutions:
  1. Check for error message: It tells you what's required (read it carefully!)
  2. Phase 1: Make sure all fields marked with * are filled in
  3. Phase 3:
    • Units must total exactly 100%
    • Grade categories must total exactly 100%
    • You must have at least 1 unit and 1 grade category
  4. Try refreshing the page (F5)
  5. Go back to the previous phase and then forward again

❌ Data disappeared / lost all my work

Problem: You had filled in Phase 1-3, but now everything is gone
Likely Cause: Browser cache was cleared
Solutions:
  1. Check if it's a different browser: Each browser has separate storage. If you use Chrome at home and Edge at school, data won't sync
  2. Export more frequently: After completing Phase 3 and 4, export your work as a backup ZIP file
  3. If you exported before losing data: You can manually re-enter from the exported file (it's slower but better than starting over)
  4. In the future: Use the same browser and don't clear cache while working
Recovery tip: Check your Downloads folder — the ZIP file you exported is a complete backup!

❌ Export button not working / file won't download

Problem: Click export option but nothing happens or download fails
Solutions:
  1. Check your browser's download settings — downloads may be blocked
  2. Look for a yellow notification bar at the top of browser (download blocked)
  3. Allow downloads for this website in browser settings
  4. Try a different browser
  5. Try a different export format (HTML instead of DOCX)
  6. Make sure you have enough disk space
  7. Check if a popup blocker is preventing download dialog
Safari users: Safari has more restrictions on downloads. Try Chrome if this is the issue.

❌ DOCX file won't open / corrupted

Problem: Downloaded DOCX file, but Word says it's corrupted
Solutions:
  1. Try opening in Google Docs instead (usually more forgiving)
  2. Try HTML export instead — save as PDF from browser
  3. Re-export the DOCX file (may have been incomplete download)
  4. Check Downloads folder — file may not have downloaded completely
  5. Try different browser to export (Chrome or Edge)

❌ Canvas paste not working

Problem: Exported HTML and tried to paste into Canvas, but formatting is broken
Solutions:
  1. Use Canvas HTML editor: Click "Edit Page" → HTML Editor (not Rich Content Editor)
  2. Clear formatting: In Canvas, use "Remove Formatting" before pasting
  3. Copy whole HTML: Make sure you copied the entire HTML file content
  4. Try as link instead: Save HTML file to Google Drive and link to it
  5. Use DOCX instead: Export as DOCX, open in Word, copy section by section into Canvas

❌ Pacing guide weeks look wrong

Problem: The CSV pacing guide's start/end weeks don't match your actual calendar
Solutions:
  1. Check your start and end dates in Phase 1 — make sure they're correct
  2. Pacing is based on the "Weeks (Est.)" value you set for each unit in Phase 3 — adjust it there if a unit needs more or less time
  3. Since this generator doesn't track a class calendar or holidays, you're responsible for accounting for breaks and non-instructional time when estimating each unit's weeks

❌ Browser back button goes away from app

Problem: Clicking browser back button (← arrow) closes the app instead of going back a phase
Solution: Always use the app's "← Back" button inside the form, not your browser's back button. The app has its own back button that works correctly.

✅ Still having issues?

Next steps:
  1. Try a completely fresh start: Clear browser cache → close browser → reopen
  2. Try a different browser (Chrome is most reliable)
  3. Use a local server instead of direct file open
  4. Check that JavaScript is enabled in your browser
  5. Update your browser to the latest version

If issues persist after these steps, there may be a browser compatibility problem. Contact your IT department or try on a different computer.

⭐Best Practices & Tips

💡 Tip 1: Start with your planning documents

Before opening the app, gather:

  • Your program of studies (PDF or curriculum document)
  • Your grading rubric or existing syllabus

Why: You can upload your program of studies to auto-populate unit data, saving time.

💡 Tip 2: Export early and often

Don't wait until the very end to export. Instead:

  • After Phase 3 → Export a backup
  • After Phase 4 → Export another backup
  • Final export → Get all formats (ZIP)

Why: If something goes wrong, you have backups to recover from.

💡 Tip 3: Use the CSV pacing export for planning

After you finish, export as CSV and open in Excel. You now have:

  • A clear breakdown of each unit and its timing
  • Start and end weeks for each unit
  • A document to share with department colleagues

Why: Helps with lesson planning and coordinating with other teachers.

💡 Tip 4: Be specific with policies

Instead of generic policies like "No cheating," be specific:

❌ Vague: Students must be respectful
✅ Specific: Raise hand before speaking, use respectful tone, listen to classmates without interrupting

Why: Students know exactly what you expect, reducing misunderstandings.

💡 Tip 5: Customize the exported DOCX

The DOCX file is a starting point. You can:

  • Add your school logo
  • Change colors and fonts to match school branding
  • Insert images or graphics
  • Add a course schedule table
  • Include required textbook information

Why: Makes your syllabus more professional and visually appealing to students.

💡 Tip 6: Keep percentages realistic

When distributing percentages across units:

  • Don't make all units equal if some are more important
  • Consider difficulty: Complex units might get 30%, simpler ones 20%
  • Early units (review): 10-15%
  • Core units: 20-30% each
  • Final unit (application): 15-25%

Why: Signals to students what's most important and helps with time allocation.

💡 Tip 7: Update your syllabus each year

Use the exported files as templates for next year:

  • Save a copy named "Bio30_2026-2027_Draft.docx"
  • Update dates and student cohort info
  • Re-export and you're done

Why: Saves time in future years; you're not starting from scratch.

💡 Tip 8: Share a copy with colleagues

After creating your syllabus, share the DOCX or HTML with department colleagues. They can:

  • Use it as a template for their courses
  • Ensure consistency across the department
  • Adapt policies and structures to fit their courses

Why: Speeds up syllabus creation for your whole department.
